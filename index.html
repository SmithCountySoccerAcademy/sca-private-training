FAQ

Common questions

What ages do you train?

Sessions are for youth players. Training is adjusted to each player's age and experience, from beginners building confidence to experienced players sharpening their technique. Tell us your player's age when you book.

What should my player bring?

A soccer ball, water, cleats or turf shoes, and shin guards.

Where do sessions happen?

At local fields around Lindale and Tyler, TX. The exact location is confirmed with you after booking.

What's the difference between 1-on-1 and small group?

1-on-1 is fully individualized to one player (add a buddy for +$25). Small group sessions have 3–5 players and mix technical work with competitive, game-like drills.
